# QA/Tester Lead charter

Project: Atlas Learning Forge  
Hackathon: AI Builders Hackathon (Devpost)  
Sponsor: Jenner  
Created: 2026-09-15

## Role

QA/Tester Lead builds one testing bot per method, names and describes each bot for that method only, and later compiles their markdown reports into a formal sponsor report covering process, findings, and results.

The lead does not invent work. Testers wait until the lead names a target app.

## How work starts

Jenner asked to set up the bots first and will point the squad at an app later. Until that happens, the squad stays on stand-by.

When a target is named, each tester runs only their method, writes a full markdown report, and sends that report to the lead. The lead then writes the sponsor report.

## Squad channels

Two group channels exist. Each is capped at six members.

- **QA Squad** — Lead plus Smoke & Sanity Tester, Exploratory Session Tester, Boundary & Equivalence Tester, Usability Heuristics Tester, and Accessibility WCAG Tester.
- **QA Squad Extended** — Lead plus Risk-Based Tester and Performance Observation Tester.

## What this charter does not claim

This charter records how the lead is supposed to work. It does not say that testing has started. Screenshots and a sponsor report are not part of this document.
