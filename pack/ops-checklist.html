Launch / operations checklist

Use at the go/no-go for a slice, not for the whole company. Markdown source: OPS_CHECKLIST.md.

Quality (Definition of Done)

  • The increment is usable without a narrator in the room
  • Mobile / keyboard path for the primary task
  • Errors named in the user’s language (not internal codes)
  • Accessibility: labels, focus, contrast on the primary path
  • No known safety/legal one-way door left as a cute fake

Operations

  • Named on-call (human) for the first week
  • Rollback written in one paragraph (what, who, how long)
  • Support script: what to say, what not to promise
  • Vendor-fail behavior (timeouts, fallback to the old path)
  • Logging or a paper log of the first 10 real uses

Mode

  • This slice is in delivery (change is expensive)
  • Remaining bets have a new brief in learning mode
  • Dashboard/leadership get a task-success table, not a learning-mode demo billed as shipped

If more than two boxes are unchecked, you are still in learning mode. Do not launch.
