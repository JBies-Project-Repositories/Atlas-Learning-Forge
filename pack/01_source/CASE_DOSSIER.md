# Case dossier — Contractor onboarding (through-line)

One case, four arc-packs. Read this after Arc D.

## The job

New contractors cannot finish required tax/onboarding forms without Slack-DMing staff. Leadership asked for “AI summaries / nicer onboarding” and a demo date.

## What is true after each arc

| Arc | What we know | What we do **not** yet know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | The portal/job is **complex**; the payroll-file cutover on the same program is **complicated**. Friday’s artifact must be a loop, not a fake six-month Gantt. | Whether contractors can find the packet. |
| B | Discovery and delivery must split. Two DoDs. Intent: purpose = unassisted completion; non-goal = no new vendor this sprint. | Pre-fill quality. |
| C | Riskiest assumption is **findability**, not helper polish. Lo-fi experience test of the real inbox → link → form. | Whether pre-fill helps after findability holds. |
| D | 4/5 failed findability → kill the pretty AI demo as a *test*. Ship the findability slice (better link, support script, rollback). Pre-fill stays in **learning mode** with a new brief. | Live ops load after launch of the findability slice. |

## Named moves used

Diverge (no scoring) → dual-track sandwich → pretotype → RAT fail → redesign findability → ship slice → mode switch.

## One-page brief (final)

- **Purpose:** A new contractor finishes required forms in one sitting without DMing staff.
- **End state:** 4/5 observed trials complete unassisted on a phone.
- **Constraints:** No new vendor; existing identity provider; accessibility of current stack.
- **Non-goals:** Marketing rewrite; AI helper this round.
- **Success:** Task success on findability, then a separate threshold for any pre-fill Wizard-of-Oz.
- **Ops at ship:** Support script, rollback = revert the link, named on-call for first five tickets.
