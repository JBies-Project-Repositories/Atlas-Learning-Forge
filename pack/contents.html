Four arc-packs

The cover was the introduction. This page is the atlas: sixteen lessons in four mini-packs, then quiz and labs. A week per arc is a reasonable path; a map-skim is not the point.

Each arc is as deep as a study pack. Primary sources, facilitation scripts, and a continuing contractor-onboarding case sit inside the lessons.

Suggested path
  1. Arc A (L01–L04) → Spaced Repetition
  2. Arc B (L05–L08) → Term Match
  3. Arc C (L09–L12) → Scenario Audit
  4. Arc D (L13–L16) → Steelman + quiz; then case dossier and ops checklist
