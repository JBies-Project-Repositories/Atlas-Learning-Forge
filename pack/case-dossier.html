The job

New contractors cannot finish required tax/onboarding forms without Slack-DMing staff. Leadership asked for “AI summaries / nicer onboarding” and a demo date.

One-page brief (final)

  • Purpose: A new contractor finishes required forms in one sitting without DMing staff.
  • End state: 4/5 observed trials complete unassisted on a phone.
  • Constraints: No new vendor; existing identity provider; accessibility of the current stack.
  • Non-goals: Marketing rewrite; AI helper this round.
  • Success: Task success on findability, then a separate threshold for any pre-fill Wizard-of-Oz.
  • Ops at ship: Support script, rollback = revert the link, named on-call for first five tickets.
